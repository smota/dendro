Model & Harness Message Mix

Counts of message metadata by harness, session, role, direction, and modality. Bodies are not captured. I02 accounts disjoint tokens. I08 accounts settled action failure. Neither number is recomputed on this page.

I02 model.tokens.direct

Disjoint sums of input_uncached, cache_write, cache_read, and output. Reasoning is included in output and is not part of the disjoint sum. Cache-read ratio of sums and mean of ratios are separate measures.

I08 harness.action_failure

Failed divided by settled terminal actions. Unsettled rows, where failed is null, are counted apart and are never treated as succeeded. This view does not store tool arguments.
