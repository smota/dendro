Compiled Insights Strip (insight_v1)

Six v0.8.0 reference insights. L0 is the domain disclosure. Status is the insight_v1 contract. This strip does not recompute measures.

I02 L0 · Model
model.tokens.direct

Status: Computed when usage rows exist; Refused on an empty population or a failed store read. Formula: disjoint sums of input_uncached, cache_write, cache_read, and output per model. Reasoning stays inside output and is not added again. cache_read_ratio_of_sums and cache_read_mean_of_ratios are separate measures.

I04 L0 · Model
model.cost

Status: Computed, or Partial when observed or estimated USD is unknown. Formula: observed_usd and estimated_usd stay on separate measures and are never added. Unknown is not zero. The pricing table ships empty, so estimates stay unknown.

I08 L0 · Harness
harness.action_failure

Status: Computed, or Partial when unsettled actions remain. Formula: failed divided by settled terminal actions (failed IS NOT NULL). Unsettled actions (failed IS NULL) are counted separately and are never treated as success.

I09 L0 · Coordination
work.lineage_shape

Status: Computed, or Partial when a parent is unresolved or a cycle stops the walk. Formula: explicit session_edges only. Relations include spawn, delegate, handoff, and return. Roots, depth, and diamonds are measured. Cycles are flagged. Unresolved parents are counted and never invented as roots.

I14 L0 · Environment
environment.resources

Status: Computed when identities and samples exist; Refused on an empty population. Formula: CPU deltas and max sampled RSS. Per root_key and harness, sum CPU deltas on readable non-decreasing samples and take the max sampled RSS per rss_unit, with launched versus discovered attribution. RSS units are never mixed.

I18 L0 · Environment
coverage.source

Status: Computed for an unwindowed scope; Refused when a from or to bound is set. Formula: read, projected, unprojected-by-reason, and refused counts per source. A missing source is not a zero.
