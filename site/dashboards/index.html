Compiled Insights Strip (insight_v1)

Catalog index I01–I25. L0 is status, finding, practical meaning, scope, and missingness. This strip does not recompute measures.

I01 L0 · Model
model.invocations

Count distinct evidenced call IDs. Usage-only rows are separate. No admitted call facts → not_observable. The presenter does not recompute this measure.

I02 L0 · Model
model.tokens.direct

Status: Computed when usage rows exist; Refused on an empty population or a failed store read. Formula: disjoint sums of input_uncached, cache_write, cache_read, and output per model. Reasoning stays inside output and is not added again. cache_read_ratio_of_sums and cache_read_mean_of_ratios are separate measures.

I03 L0 · Model
model.tokens.inclusive

Inclusive sum over the reachable observation union. Nonadditive. Incomplete nonnegative lineage is a lower bound. Ambiguous duplicates refuse the canonical total.

I04 L0 · Model
model.cost

Status: Computed, or Partial when observed or estimated USD is unknown. Formula: observed_usd and estimated_usd stay on separate measures and are never added. Unknown is not zero. The pricing table ships empty, so estimates stay unknown.

I05 L0 · Model
model.configuration_divergence

Observed bindings that differ from contemporaneous desired bindings, or from paired observed bindings. Unknown values are excluded and counted. Missing paired population → not_observable.

I06 L0 · Harness
harness.capability_funnel

Distinct loaded and invoked capability IDs, and the invoked∩loaded / loaded rate. Absent loaded evidence refuses the rate. Actions still count.

I07 L0 · Harness
harness.context_pressure

Evidenced context-used / context-limit at invocation. Prompt tokens do not substitute. A missing limit refuses the ratio.

I08 L0 · Harness
harness.action_failure

Status: Computed, or Partial when unsettled actions remain. Formula: failed divided by settled terminal actions (failed IS NOT NULL). Unsettled actions (failed IS NULL) are counted separately and are never treated as success.

I09 L0 · Coordination
work.lineage_shape

Status: Computed, or Partial when a parent is unresolved or a cycle stops the walk. Formula: explicit session_edges only. Relations include spawn, delegate, handoff, and return. Roots, depth, and diamonds are measured. Cycles are flagged. Unresolved parents are counted and never invented as roots.

I10 L0 · Coordination
work.duration

Wall union, summed attempt-seconds, and summed/wall. Half-open same-clock segments. A censored contribution is partial. wall=0 refuses the concurrency ratio.

I11 L0 · Coordination
work.shared_allocation

Shared usage, weighted beneficiaries, and residual. Rational weights are ≥ 0 and sum ≤ 1. Absent policy leaves the shared bucket.

I12 L0 · Coordination
coord.queue_wait

Sum or median of ready-to-start elapsed intervals, and a separate wall union. Missing ready, start, or cross-clock uncertainty refuses the affected intervals.

I13 L0 · Environment
environment.placement

Distinct incarnations and placement intervals by locus, plus same-label rebuild count. An unknown incarnation gets an unresolved count.

I14 L0 · Environment
environment.resources

Status: Computed when identities and samples exist; Refused on an empty population. Formula: CPU deltas and max sampled RSS. Per root_key and harness, sum CPU deltas on readable non-decreasing samples and take the max sampled RSS per rss_unit, with launched versus discovered attribution. RSS units are never mixed.

I15 L0 · Environment
environment.effects

Normalized successful and failed syscall counts by operation and resource class. An unmatched call has an unknown result. No trace → not_observable.

I16 L0 · Security
security.isolation

Paired declared / configured / tested dimension comparisons. A lone declaration is insufficient. Namespace equality is configuration, not a universal verification.

I17 L0 · Security
security.authority

Comparator counts of consistent, mismatch, and insufficient by an explicit rule. No applicable policy → NOT_ASSESSED, never a zero violation count.

I18 L0 · Environment
coverage.source

Status: Computed for an unwindowed scope; Refused when a from or to bound is set. Formula: read, projected, unprojected-by-reason, and refused counts per source. A missing source is not a zero.

I19 L0 · Coordination
outcome.acceptance

Currently accepted eligible work / rule-eligible evaluated work, with attempt success kept separate. No acceptance rule → not_observable.

I20 L0 · Coordination
outcome.resource_efficiency

Matched-population cost / accepted work, and a separate mean of per-work ratios. Zero accepted is null, not infinity. Cost stays on I04.

I21 L0 · Model
comparison.descriptive

Stratified difference of same-version measures. Match clock basis, grain, accounting, units, and coverage. Biased or unmatched selection → noncomparable.

I22 L0 · Harness
harness.trajectory_vortex

Distance from the cohort centroid of completed sessions. A trip is not a cause. L1 assumptions block that reading. The SQL trigger only marks the session dirty.

I23 L0 · Harness
harness.schema_drift

L1 distance of envelope_shape key-path and variant_type distributions, plus keys added, removed, and retyped. A short sample is reason insufficient_sample and badge insufficient_evidence.

I24 L0 · Security
security.authority_blast_radius

Downstream sessions on status = evidenced edges only. The count is dependency exposure, not inherited or effective authority. PageRank is not an input.

I25 L0 · Model
model.counterfactual_routing

Simulated fast-tier cost and context pressure from an operator-pinned tier table. It is not an effect and not a routing change. An unpinned cohort or tier profile refuses the simulation and keeps observed I02.
