Deep Observability

The Three Critical Operational Layers

Engineered specifically to eliminate the operational blind spots that conventional APM tools ignore when managing autonomous fleets.

01 Question 1

Seat Dynamics & Yield Ratio

"Are my seats over-provisioned? Who is actively producing vs. sitting idle while work stacks up in the queue?"

  • 5 Disjoint Activity Bands: Active, idle-with-work, idle-without-work, waiting-human, unknown.
  • Exact Conservation Law: Mathematical certainty across the entire observation window.
  • Over-Engineering Detection: Surfaces intermediate text-passing seats that add zero value.
02 Question 2

Handover Flow & Build Rework

"How much context is lost during handoffs? How often do review seats bounce items back to builders?"

  • Rework Chains: Pinpoints review-to-builder returns along task item histories.
  • Intake Token Overhead: Measures token rehydration costs for downstream seats.
  • Queue Wait Latency: Quantifies time lost between task creation and seat pickup.
03 Question 3

Token Burn, Costs & Retry Loops

"Where are tokens burning? Is an agent trapped in a repetitive, failing command loop?"

  • Multi-Model Attribution: Ingests tokens and provider costs by mission and seat.
  • Zero-Yield Loop Detection: Identifies failing tool sequences that produce zero diff.
  • Deterministic Spend Ledger: Reconciles prompt, completion, and cache tokens.
