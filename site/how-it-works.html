How Dendro Works

From scattered local session logs to a 4-layer causal X-ray.

Every AI coding harness leaves behind local session files on disk. Dendro discovers and ingests those footprints out-of-band into an embedded columnar DuckDB engine—classifying activity across 4 Canonical Architectural Layers and the 5-Layer Capability & Execution Matrix.

Step 01 Passive Local Discovery

Zero proxies, zero prompts, zero secrets

Dendro reads existing transcripts from Claude Code, OpenAI Codex, Google AGY, xAI Grok CLI, and Pi (plus optional multi-agent orchestration adapters) directly on disk. Secret patterns (sk-, ghp_, xai-) are scrubbed before storage.

Step 02 Dual Taxonomy & Schema 5

4 Architectural Layers × 5 Capability Layers

Sessions are partitioned into five disjoint activity bands (active, waiting_on_human, idle_with_work, idle_without_work, unknown), 5-part token accounting (I02), FUNNEL I06 skills conversion, and 25 compiled insights (I01–I25).

Step 03 Ratatui TUI, Web Studio & Nao

Inspect in terminal, browser, or plain English

Explore in the 6-screen Ratatui cockpit (dendro cockpit), serve the 12-view Connected Studio on loopback (dendro dashboard), or query 12 deterministic Nao views with dendro ask.

Epistemic Honesty Boundaries

What Dendro proves—and what it refuses to fabricate.

Instrument-grade telemetry starts by naming what local evidence can establish and where mathematical boundaries sit.

Dimension What Dendro reports from local evidence What Dendro never fabricates
Null ≠ Zero Five exclusive status badges (ok, partial, insufficient_evidence, refused, not_applicable) and hatched unobserved intervals. Treating missing tables or unobserved intervals as zero activity.
Tokens ≠ Productivity 5-part token breakdown (input_uncached, cache_write, cache_read, output, nested reasoning) and 96.9% cache read yield. Correlating high token burn with high engineering output or developer value.
Observed vs. Simulated Telemetry Direct DuckDB fact views and clearly labeled counterfactual simulations across I22–I25 (such as SIMULATED TIER TOTAL (COUNTERFACTUAL)). Presenting counterfactual routing simulations (I25) as live router mutations or guaranteed savings.
Privacy & Exfiltration Offline HTML explorer with local actor aliasing (Actor 1, Actor 2) and redaction of paths, prompts, and tokens. Persisting raw prompts, thoughts, or tool payloads, or uploading telemetry to any cloud server.
