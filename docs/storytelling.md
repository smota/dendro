# The Story Behind Dendro

**Your fleet has a history. Now you can read it.**

A tree records its life in growth rings. Seasons of abundance, drought, and recovery leave a pattern that becomes legible when you know where to look. Dendro brings that idea to AI engineering: execution records become a history of how your fleet worked, where it struggled, and what deserves your attention next.

## From scattered activity to a clear story

An agent team can be busy in every terminal while the work barely moves. One session repeats a failing command. Another waits for a handoff. A third consumes context rebuilding what the previous actor already knew. Each harness records a fragment of the story; the operator needs the whole picture.

Dendro connects those fragments across Claude Code, Codex, AGY, Grok, Pi, and OpenRig. Model consumption, harness behavior, coordination, and environment become layers of one investigation. You can see the pattern, locate the interval, and follow it to the records that support a decision.

## Begin with the question that matters

An operator opens Dendro because something needs a decision:

- **“Where did the tokens go?”** Separate fresh input, cache activity, and output, then follow the concentration to a harness, workspace, or outcome.
- **“Why did this work lose momentum?”** Inspect tool loops, queue wait, and handoff returns around the same interval.
- **“Are our capabilities reaching execution?”** Compare declared skills with invocations and inspect the primitives, MCP tools, and hooks doing the work.
- **“What changed after the upgrade?”** Examine structural shifts in harness records and focus the next connector check.
- **“What should we try next?”** Explore routing and topology scenarios against recorded work before choosing a change.

The question gives the evidence a purpose. A total becomes useful when it helps the operator choose a next step.

## Read the rings, then pull the stack apart

The **Living Growth Rings** make fleet activity legible at a glance. The **Time Machine** lets you move through the period that matters, and **Story Highlights** turn patterns into concise findings with a path to the underlying layer.

When a finding needs context, the **Stack X-Ray** separates **Model → Harness → Coordination → Environment**. Follow consumption into tool behavior, tool behavior into handoffs, and handoffs into resource and permission observations. Keep the same interval while changing your perspective.

The **5-Layer Capability & Execution Intelligence** view adds another question: what did the agents actually use? **Tool Families**, **Harness Primitives**, **Skills**, **MCP Servers & Tools**, and **Lifecycle Hooks & Guardrails** reveal the balance between available capability and observed execution.

## Turn a signal into a decision

A useful fleet narrative has a simple progression:

1. **Name the change.** “Token consumption became concentrated in one harness during the release window.”
2. **Locate the supporting behavior.** Inspect the tool activity and handoff flow in that interval.
3. **Choose the next action.** Review the affected session, adjust a handoff, or test a routing scenario.
4. **Return to the evidence.** Compare a later window to see what changed.

Active Intelligence helps focus that progression. **Trajectory Vortex** surfaces repeated behavior. **Schema Drift** highlights changes in record structure. **Authority Blast Radius** maps dependency exposure. **Counterfactual Model Routing** lets the operator explore alternatives against history.

## Tell the story for the person making the decision

| Audience | Lead with | Help them decide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Applied-AI Engineer** | The session or tool pattern that lost momentum. | Where to investigate first. |
| **Fleet Architect** | The handoff, capability gap, or version change shaping execution. | What to simplify or redesign. |
| **FinOps / Token Economist** | Consumption components and where they concentrate. | Which budget or routing assumption to examine. |
| **Security & Governance Lead** | Permission observations and downstream dependencies. | Which boundary or exposure needs review. |

Use concrete language: “This handoff returned for rework” or “These skills were available but had no recorded invocation in the selected period.” Attach a number when it changes the decision, and keep the observation window clear.

## Trust is part of the product

A clear narrative rests on evidence. Dendro keeps gaps visible, labels simulations, and lets operators inspect the supporting records. A repeated pattern invites investigation; the explanation comes from following the evidence.

Privacy makes that investigation practical. Dendro works locally, reads source logs without changing them, and stores operational facts while keeping prompts, thoughts, responses, tool payloads, and secrets out of the analytics store. Fleet governance stays with the operator.
