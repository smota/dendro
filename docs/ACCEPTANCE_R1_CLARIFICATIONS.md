| K5 | 2026-10-10 22:00 | Historical destination of a queue item (K2 closed items, K3 in-progress items): when the current `queue_items` value may be used | The destination at instant t is taken from the latest destination-bearing event at or before t: `queue.created` (`destinationSession`), `qitem.fallback_routed` (`rerouteDestination`); `queue.claimed` carries the same value and may confirm it.
